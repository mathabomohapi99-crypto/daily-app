# CampusFlow — Epics

- **Board & Card Core** — the basic Board → Column → Card structure: creating, viewing, moving, and deleting cards across To Do / In Progress / Done.
- **Task Categorization** — tagging cards by type (Assignment, Test, Activity) and by subject/module, so items can be filtered and sorted.
- **Due Dates & Reminders** — attaching due dates to cards and surfacing upcoming or overdue items.
- **Progress Overview** — a simple summary view showing how many tasks are done vs pending, per subject or overall.
- **Lecturer Posting (future)** — a later-stage epic allowing lecturers to log in and post assignments/tests directly, instead of the student adding them manually.
